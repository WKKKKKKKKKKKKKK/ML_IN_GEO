5. Change the sequence length to 16 and re-evaluate the models' performance
============================================================

Best model at each sequence length

Effect of increasing sequence length from 8 to 16

Unidirectional models
- RNN: RMSE improved from 12.1102 to 9.3922, and R2 improved from 0.8802 to 0.9301.
- GRU: RMSE improved from 11.2701 to 6.6457, and R2 improved from 0.8962 to 0.9650.
- LSTM: RMSE improved from 10.6406 to 6.7129, and R2 improved from 0.9075 to 0.9643.

Bidirectional models
- BiRNN: RMSE improved from 11.9695 to 8.0919, and R2 improved from 0.8830 to 0.9481.
- BiGRU: RMSE improved from 12.3207 to 7.1265, and R2 improved from 0.8760 to 0.9598.
- BiLSTM: RMSE improved from 10.1331 to 6.3133, and R2 improved from 0.9161 to 0.9684.

Interpretation
- Increasing the sequence length to 16 improved every model tested in this assignment.
- The improvement suggests that a longer temporal context helps the network capture the relationships needed for DTSM prediction.
- The strongest overall configuration after re-evaluation remained BiLSTM with sequence length 16.

============================================================
Conclusion
============================================================

- The optimal hyperparameters repeatedly favored base_hidden_size=16 and learning_rate=0.0010.
- GRU preferred 4 recurrent layers, while RNN and LSTM preferred 2 layers.
- Bidirectional layers were beneficial for RNN and LSTM, but not for GRU on this dataset.
- Sequence length 16 outperformed sequence length 8 for every model.
- The final recommended model for this assignment is BiLSTM with sequence length 16.
